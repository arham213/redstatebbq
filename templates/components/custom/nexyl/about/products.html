every sauce has a story

Apple bourbon

It started with Jenny Evans at Evans Orchard, who kept asking for an apple sauce for pork until the owner finally gave in. The blend of apple, Kentucky bourbon, maple, and hickory smoke turned out better than expected and it's been a customer favorite ever since.

Taproom Fire

Born after the Georgetown taproom burned down: a blend of six sauces spiked with Country Boy "Cougar Bait" beer and an extra hit of habanero. A special edition that came out of the ashes.
